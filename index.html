The Story

Fewer escapes. Faster releases.

I'm a Principal SQA Engineer and QA Lead with 10+ years across fintech, banking, insurance, healthcare and e-commerce. I own end-to-end QA strategy, release sign-off and defect management across concurrent client engagements.

Today I lead QA at KAZ Software, where 400+ automated suites and a sharper regression strategy have held defect leakage below 2% for two years, keeping a bi-weekly release cadence steady for enterprise customers including Accenture and Wipro.

  • Strategy before test cases

    Coverage follows risk. I plan around what can actually hurt the release, then scope regression with change-impact analysis.

  • Automation that earns its keep

    Selenium, Mabl and Postman/Newman wired into CI/CD with build alerts, so regression runs itself and people test what's new.

  • Sign-off you can trust

    I own the release decision. Microservices on Amazon EKS get validated with Grafana dashboards and CloudWatch logs, not hope.

  • Teams that outlast rotations

    Certified Scrum Master and Agile coach. I mentor engineers, run onboarding and keep quality steady when people change.
